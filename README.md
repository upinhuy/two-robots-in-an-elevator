Entry for the [Two Robots in an Elevator](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-two-robots-in-an-elevator-8c6aeb8f) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun/r/hv9zk8y5) — one funny line of awkward small talk a robot says to another while they ride an elevator together.

## The entry

> I simulated this elevator ride 4,000 times. I still have no idea what to do with my arms. Do you have arms?

A robot that has rehearsed the ride thousands of times in simulation still
freezes on the most human part of it — what to do with its arms — and then
asks the other robot whether it even has any. Machine logic, awkward
silence, and a surprise turn in a single line.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (107 characters, within
the 1–200 character gate). Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
